## Title

ModelScope — Scientific Models Have Limits | Hackathon Demo

## Description

ModelScope helps students inspect where scientific measurements become systematically inconsistent with a configured model. AI explains. The analysis engine decides.

Live demo: https://modelscope-ten.vercel.app/
Source: https://github.com/luiscontrerasus-glitch/ModelScope

Built by Luis Contreras, solo, during the overlapping eligible hackathon period. Codex assisted development; Gemini provides reviewed setup proposals and optional explanations of deterministic evidence. Built-in demonstrations are synthetic educational data. Transition sensitivity is not a confidence interval or proof of a physical mechanism.

This 2:45 demo shows the actual public production application in a 1920×1080 export with burned-in captions and local installed Windows synthesized narration. No music or mock AI. Capture emits frames when the page changes; output is 30 FPS and does not claim 60 FPS acquisition. Normal scientific calculations and raw measurements stay in the browser; optional AI shares minimal requested context.
